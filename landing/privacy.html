Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Moyo is an early-stage product currently in testing. This policy explains what we collect on this site today and how we handle it. We'll update this as the product grows.

What we collect

On this waitlist page, we collect only what you enter in the form:

  • Your email address
  • The category you're checking (e.g. internet, insurance)
  • The provider name and amount you tell us you're paying

We do not currently collect bank account information, passwords, or any financial credentials through this public site. If that changes as the product develops, this policy will be updated first, and you'll be asked for clear consent before any account-connection feature goes live for you.

How we use it

We use your email to follow up with the results of your Leak Report check and to let you know when Moyo is ready for broader access. We do not use it for anything else.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information to anyone.
  • We don't share it with advertisers.
  • We don't use it to train unrelated products.

Where it's stored

Waitlist submissions are processed through Formspree, a third-party form service. You can review their own privacy practices at their website. We may migrate to different infrastructure as the product matures.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your information at any time by contacting us (see below). Since we're early-stage, this is currently a manual process on our end, but we'll honor every request.

Changes to this policy

As Moyo adds real account connections and more functionality, this policy will expand significantly, particularly around how financial data is accessed, stored, and protected. We'll highlight material changes rather than updating silently.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: hello@moyoapp.co
